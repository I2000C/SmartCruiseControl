# Requirements

## Software

- VSCode (https://code.visualstudio.com/)
- PlatformIO extension installed on VSCode (https://platformio.org/install/ide?install=vscode).

## Hardware

- Wires
- Soldering iron (and related soldering stuff)
- A small switch (to safely disconnect power supply if something unexpected happens)
- Male USB to wires
- Female USB to wires
- SN65HVD230
- HK19F-DC 5v-SHG (other DPDT relays may work)
- IRLZ44N x1 (other MOSFETs may work)
- 1N4003 x1 (other flyback diodes may work)
- 2N3904 x4 (other NPN transistors may work)
- 100uF 16V capacitor x2
- 220 resistor x1
- 1k resistor x3
- 5k resistor x1
- 10k resistor x1
- 47k resistor x4
- 100k resistor x1
- ESP32 devkit v1 board

<img src="https://docs.zephyrproject.org/latest/_images/doit_esp32_devkit_v1.webp" alt="esp32 board" width="200">

- Male OBDII connector to wires, including at least **GND**, **CAN low** and **CAN high**

<img src="https://ae-pic-a1.aliexpress-media.com/kf/S3273a3d494064d3ba4c032014ecd8da3h.jpg" alt="obdII connector to wires" width="200"/>

- Throttle connector (reference: XG7061C-1.5-11/21 1473284-1)

<img src="https://ae-pic-a1.aliexpress-media.com/kf/Sa9a7d60d9bab4962a76a4a4b33f772daZ.jpg" alt="XG7061C automotive 6-hole connector" width="200"/>

- JST-XH 2.54mm connector kit

<img src="https://ae-pic-a1.aliexpress-media.com/kf/Sef9454ba1f224f38a720db11ede32f483.jpg" alt="JST-XH connector kit" width="200"/>
