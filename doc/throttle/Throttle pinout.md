# Throttle pinouts

## Pinouts
- **UPWG2** (yellow/red wire) = 2.38V (~= 2.5V)
- **UPWG1** (yellow/green wire) = 5V
- **PWG1** (gray wire) = APPS1
- **GND1** (brown/pink wire) = GND
- **GND2** (brown wire) = GND
- **PWG2** (black/pink wire) = APPS2

## Voltage range (measured with multimeter)
- **APPS1**: 0.9772V (0%) --> 3.174V (100%)
- **APPS2**: 0.4588V (0%) --> 1.4850V (100%)

## Relation between APPS 1 and APPS2
APPS1 / APPS2 ~= 2.13

NOTE: APPS means "Accelerator Pedal Position Sensor"
